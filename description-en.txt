Format > Make It Fit...  fits a paper into a page limit. Choose the number of pages and a standard (normal printing, APA 7th edition, or Chicago/Turabian). The space between paragraphs, the margins and the line spacing are adjusted as little as possible, and never beyond the standard's limits. If the paper cannot fit within the standard, it is fitted to the next page count that works and you are told. One Undo puts everything back.

Insert > Suppress Numbering on First Page  leaves the title page without a page number and starts counting at 1 on the next page. Choose it again to undo.
